SniffNet is a fictional social media platform set in 2060, where humans have already figured out a way to translate animal languages into human languages and then translate it back to animal languages, and animals can have their own online identities, express their personalities, and communicate with each other. I wanted to imagine a future internet that is not only designed for humans, but also gives animals a voice and a space to connect.

For this project, I created four interconnected pages: an Explore page, a personal profile page, a Friends page, and a private messaging page. Together, they demonstrate the basic functions of a social media platform, including posting, liking, commenting, following, and messaging. My main character is one of my own cats called Pineapple, a cat with his own personality, interests, and friendships.

I chose a warm yellow color palette because pineapple is yellow. I also added small stickers of his favorite objects, such as toys and treats, to make the interface feel more personal. I wanted the design to reflect the animal using the platform rather than follow one universal style. For example, if I were designing a profile for a snake, I might use a cooler, more mysterious aesthetic, while a dog's profile might feel more energetic and outgoing.

During today's class, I shared my project and received feedback from my classmates. More than one person mentioned that it would be fun if they could register their own accounts and create profiles pretending that they are they are animals. I really liked this idea, but building a complete registration system is beyond my current technical abilities and the time available for this assignment. I also considered creating multiple profile pages for different animals, such as a dog or a hamster, each with a unique visual style. However, I eventually decided to focus on making one complete demo instead.

This feedback helped me see how SniffNet could grow beyond my original design. Although the current website is only a prototype, I hope it communicates the possibility of a more inclusive future internet, where technology helps us better understand animals and recognize them as individuals with their own preferences and personalities.
